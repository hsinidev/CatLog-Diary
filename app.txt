App Name: CatLog Diary
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Pet Health / Lifestyle
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free + $3.99/month Pro

Description:
A health tracking app optimized exclusively for cats to log litter-box frequency, precise weight curves, food intake, and medication intervals. Built for devoted cat owners.

Key Features:
- Multi-cat profile management (photos, breed, birthday)
- Daily litter-box log: frequency, consistency, color (health indicators)
- Weight tracking with chart over time (flag sudden changes)
- Food log: brand, portion size, wet vs dry, calories
- Medication and supplement scheduler with reminders
- Vet visit history with notes and document storage
- Heat/season cycle tracker for unspayed females
- Monthly health summary PDF export for vet appointments
- Water intake estimator based on food type

Target Audience: Cat owners aged 20-45, multi-cat households, cat breeders
Competitors: 11pets, PetDesk, VitusVet
Unique Value: Feline-specific health metrics not found in generic pet trackers
